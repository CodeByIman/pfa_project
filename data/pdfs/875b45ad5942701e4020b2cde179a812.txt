Anti-hallucinating LVLM s with RITUAL Visual hallucinations in LVLM s can occur during the decoding phase when tokens are selected based on erroneous probability distributions that do not align with the visual inputs. Our approach aims to mitigate these visual hallucinations with a simple yet effective modification to the input handling.
