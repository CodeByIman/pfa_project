BODY:
Most traditional electrocardiogram (ECG) based automated diagnosis methods are trained at population level, neglecting the customization of personalized ECGs to enhance

individual healthcare management. In this paper, we present an innovative prospective learning approach for personalized heart disease detection, which generates digital

twins of healthy individuals' anomalous ECGs and enhances the model sensitivity to the personalized symptoms. In our approach, a vector quantized

feature separator is proposed to locate and isolate the disease symptom and normal segments in ECG signals with ECG report guidance.

Experiments demonstrate that our approach not only excels in
generating high-fidelity ECG signals but also improves personalized heart
disease detection. Moreover, our approach ensures robust privacy protection,
safeguarding patient data in model development.
