BODY:
The essence lies in learning novel unified-adaptive VL representations with our modality mixer (Moda Mixer) and asymmetrical Conv Net search.
Besides empirical results, we theoretically analyze our approach to evidence its effectiveness.
Having observed the above, we introduce a novel unified-adaptive vision-language representation, aiming for SOTA VL tracking without using Transformer 2.
Specifically, we first present modality mixer, or Moda Mixer, a conceptually simple but effective module for VL interaction.
The selected feature is then fused with the original feature, using a special asymmetrical design (analyzed later in experiments), to generate the final unified VL representation.
To mitigate the gap, we propose an asymmetrical searching strategy (ASS) to adapt the unified VL representation for improvements.
Eventually, with the unified-adaptive representation, we implement the first pure CNN-based VL tracker that shows SOTA results comparable and even better than Transformer-based solutions, without bells and whistles.
Besides empirical results, we provide theoretical analysis to evidence the effectiveness of our method.

In summary, our contributions are four-fold: (i) we introduce a novel unified-adaptive vision-language representation for SOTA VL tracking; (ii) we propose the embarrassingly simple yet effective Moda Mixer for unified VL representation learning; (iii) we present ASS to adapt mixed VL representation for better tracking and (iv) using pure CNN architecture, we achieve SOTA results on multiple benchmarks .
Recently, Transformer [48] has been introduced to Siamese tracking for better interactions of visual features and greatly pushed the standard of state-of-the-art performance [7, 51, 45, 9, 33].
The work [32] first introduces linguistic description to tracking and shows that language enhances the robustness of vision-based method.
Different from these VL trackers that regard vision and language as independent cues with weak connections only at result fusion, we propose Moda Mixer to unleash the power of VL tracking by learning unified VL representation.
Neural architecture search (NAS) aims at finding the optimal design of deep network architectures [62, 40, 35, 25] and has been introduced to tracking [54, 59].
In specific, we first describe the proposed modality mixer for generating unified multimodal representation and then asymmetrical network which searches for learning adaptive VL representation.
Afterwards, we illustrate the proposed tracking framework, followed by theoretical analysis of our method.
In this work, we propose the modality mixer (dubbed Moda Mixer) to demonstrate a compact way to learn a unified vision-language representation for tracking.
3.2 Asymmetrical Search for Adaptive Vision-Language Representation Besides the fusion module, the other crucial key for vision-language tracking is how to construct the basic modeling structure.
We adopt a popular NAS model, in particular the single-path one-shot method SPOS [25], for searching the optimal structure of our purpose.
