Title: Utilizing Automated Breast Cancer Detection to Identify Spatial Distributions of Tumor Infiltrating Lymphocytes in Invasive Breast Cancer
Authors: Han Le, Rajarsi Gupta, Le Hou, Shahira Abousamra, Danielle Fassler
Year: 2019

BODY:
We have developed and evaluated convolutional neural network (CNN) analysis pipelines to generate combined maps of cancer regions and tumor infiltrating lymphocytes (TILs)

in routine diagnostic breast cancer whole slide tissue images (WSIs). We produce interactive whole slide maps that provide 1) insight about the structural patterns

and spatial distribution of lymphocytic infiltrates and 2) facilitate improved quantification of TILs. We evaluated both tumor and TIL analyses using three CNN networks

- Resnet-34, VGG16 and Inception v4, and demonstrated that the results compared favorably to those obtained by what believe are the best published methods.

We also present a customized web-based
interface that enables easy visualization and interactive exploration of
high-resolution combined Tumor-TIL maps for 1,015TCGA invasive breast cancer
cases that can be downloaded for further downstream analyses.
