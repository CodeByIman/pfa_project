BODY:
Eventually, with the advent of advanced recurrent neural network architectures such as the LSTM, we were able to achieve state-of-the-art performance in several natural language

processing tasks such as text classification and machine translation. Several advanced architectures such as the Transformer and its variants have allowed practitioners to leverage knowledge gained

from unrelated task to drastically fasten convergence and provide better performance on the target task. This survey represents an effort at providing a succinct yet

complete understanding of the recent advances in natural language processing using deep learning in with a special focus on detailing transfer learning and its potential advantages.
