3 Classification Tree Diagram Induction A classification tree diagram  represents a structured binary tree which performs a split test in its internal nodes based on some or more attribute values and predicts decision in its leaf node. The ultimate goal of classification tree induction algorithm is to end up with the smallest tree and purest leaf nodes i.e. further splitting would not increase information gain. In anomaly detection methods , this would indicate if and where an anomaly is present in the dataset while traversing from root. Euler diagrams can be an effective representation of information when they are both wellmatched and well-formed.

This paper explains why well-matched, well-formed drawing principles are insufficient, illustrates why we should study the orientation, shape and colour of Euler diagrams, and briefly describes our research approach for this endeavor. Given the importance of accuracy and the variety of contexts in which Euler diagrams are used it is necessary to understand what makes an effective choice of diagram to represent any given data set. Having this understanding will inform the development of these tools and guide users towards drawing effective Euler diagrams.
